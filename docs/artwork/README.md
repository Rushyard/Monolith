## Prompt commun exact

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original cinematic editorial illustration for Monolith, a confidential treasury web app with a 2001 space odyssey art direction. A unified series: black obsidian, fine subtle analogue film grain, warm ivory light, quiet monumental industrial design, sophisticated photorealistic miniature practical model photography, restrained and tactile, nearly black seamless backdrop and floor, landscape 4:3 composition, central subject with breathing room. No text, typography, letters, logos, numbers, crypto coins, UI, people, padlock icons, cyberpunk neon, purple, blue or rainbow. Beautiful readable silhouette, deep shadows but visible material detail.

Chaque prompt est constitué du texte commun ci-dessus suivi du texte correspondant ci-dessous.

## Coffre

Subject: a tall perfectly rectangular solid obsidian monolith, proportions width4 height9 depth1, angled three-quarter view on a dark polished floor. A single ultra-thin warm ivory line around a split vertical edge hints at light safely contained inside. Hard precise architecture, one solid structure not many panels. Soft volumetric warm beam through a narrow slit behind it, subtle stone texture. Metaphor: confidentiality, guarded treasury. Object takes up 55 percent of height, museum-like lighting.

## Agent

Subject: an original single red optical intelligence lens embedded in a broad brushed-black rectangular aerospace enclosure, tightly cropped sculptural hardware, slightly off-axis three-quarter angle. The lens has fine concentric precision glass rings and a deep vermilion red pupil with small amber-white center; one small warm ivory rim light across black metal. No other LEDs or detail clutter. Takes up 65 percent of frame height. Metaphor: autonomous intelligence contained by a hard physical boundary. HAL-like atmosphere without any wording or exact prop labels.

## Auditeur

Subject: one thick flawless smoked-glass upright rectangular slab with the same 4:9 face proportions as a monolith, on a dark floor at a three-quarter angle. A thin perfectly straight warm ivory ray enters the opaque slab at left and emerges as a precise luminous cream line at right, a restrained optical revelation, no rainbow or colours. A faint image of the light is reflected onto the floor. Sculpture occupies 55 percent of frame height. Metaphor: selective visibility, the one authorized perspective. Precise physically plausible reflections.
